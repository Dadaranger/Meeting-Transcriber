Meeting Transcriber records microphone and system audio locally, then transcribes it on this PC.

Disk planning
- The application bundle contains the offline transcription runtime.
- Speech models download only after you approve the download in the application.
- Model sizes vary from roughly 500 MB to several GB. Keep at least 5 GB free for a model, temporary processing, and meeting data.
- One hour of uncompressed dual-source audio can use about 1 GB before derived files.

Privacy and uninstall
- Meeting recordings, transcripts, and notes are stored under your Documents folder.
- Downloaded models are stored in your per-user Local App Data folder.
- Uninstall removes the application only. It deliberately leaves recordings, transcripts, notes, reviews, and downloaded models in place.
- Delete retained meeting data or models manually only after you have confirmed you no longer need them.

Recording laws vary. Tell participants and obtain any consent required where you are.
